Australian Solar Care cleans solar panels on commercial sites.

A commercial visit is the same work at a larger scale:

- The face of each panel in the array
- A rinse so cleaning residue is not left on the glass
- The site left tidy when the work is finished

Tell us the size of the array and how it is accessed, and we can quote the clean. Access requirements vary by site, so include anything the crew needs to know before arriving.
